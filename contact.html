Frequently Asked Questions

Our general support line is available 24/7. Shipping services are available Monday–Friday, 8AM–8PM GMT. Storage solutions support runs Monday–Sunday, 6AM–10PM GMT. Emergency security services are available around the clock.
For standard shipments we aim to respond within 2–4 business hours. Urgent or same-day requests are handled within 1 hour during business hours. For pre-arranged secure shipments, we can mobilise within 24 hours globally.
All our facilities hold Grade XII (EX) (CD) and EN 1143-1 certifications. We operate 24/7 armed security, biometric access controls, multi-zone CCTV, independent alarm systems, and regular third-party audits at every location.
Yes. All shipments are covered by our comprehensive all-risk transit insurance up to full market value. We work with Lloyd's of London underwriters. Specialist or high-value items can be covered under bespoke policies — contact our team for details.
